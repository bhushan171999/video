# Video brief and delivery QA

## Intake

- Project/client name; audience; main message; desired action.
- Platforms, aspect ratios, duration, resolution/FPS targets, due date.
- Source clips and preferred order; permitted trims and exclusions; language/voiceover.
- Exact copy, website/CTA, spelling and pronunciation of names.
- Approved logos, colors, fonts, reference videos, lower-third/caption style.
- Music rights, permissions for stock and AI, privacy restrictions.
- Preview-approval gate or direct final render.

Ask only for blocking items; label defaults as assumptions.

| 00:00–00:03 | Clip A, verified range | Hook | Approved exact text | Cut / music begins |

Expand to full duration. Reconcile summed timeline frames to composition duration.

## Export quality gate

1. **Technical:** Render exit success; output exists and plays; `ffprobe` confirms resolution, FPS, duration, codecs, and expected audio; no missing media/black frames.
2. **Editorial:** Compelling opening, intentional shot pacing, clean ending, accurate CTA and overlays.
3. **Brand:** Correct logo, color, typography, names, figures, claims, and no client cross-contamination.
4. **Accessibility:** Captions match speech, readable contrast and placement; intelligible speech; avoid excessive flashing.
5. **Handoff:** MP4 path, project path, duration, aspect ratio, preview if created, asset licensing caveats and outstanding issues. Preserve user originals.

Never assert an export was verified unless each relevant check actually ran.
